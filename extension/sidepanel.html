Teach PinScout how another store checks delivery.
1. Open the store's product page.
2. Type a pincode below and press Start. PinScout reloads the page once, so it's in place before the store's own code runs.
3. On the store's page, change the delivery pincode to that same number. If the store reloads or opens other pages, that's fine.
4. Press Stop & copy, then Send by email and paste the recording into the email.
Cookies, tokens, session values, addresses, names, phone numbers and e-mails are masked, but masking isn't perfect. If you're logged in, skim the result first, or record while logged out.
