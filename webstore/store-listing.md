## Description (paste as is)

```
Seeing "Currently unavailable for your pincode" or "Out of stock"? PinScout finds nearby pincodes, and places across India, where the product can be delivered.

When a shopping site says a phone, gadget or deal is out of stock or can't be delivered to your pincode, PinScout checks the areas around you for you, so you don't have to type pincode after pincode into the site's delivery box.

How it works
PinScout is a free delivery checker and pincode finder for online shopping in India. Open a product page on a supported store, type your pincode, and PinScout:
1. Checks your own pincode first.
2. If it can't be delivered there, checks nearby pincodes, a few kilometres away.
3. Then moves out to the rest of your state, and then the rest of India.
It stops at the first place the product can be delivered and shows you where, along with the delivery date the site gives.

Useful when an item can't come to you but could go to a friend, relative, office or hostel nearby.

What it does
• Out of stock or not deliverable at your pincode: finds the nearest pincode where it can be delivered
• Uses India Post data for about 19,500 pincodes, so "nearby" really means nearby
• You choose how far to search: nearby only, your state, or all of India
• Can keep going and list every place the product is available
• Runs in Chrome's side panel, so you can keep browsing the product page while it checks
• Puts your own delivery pincode back on sites that save it (Amazon)

Supported stores
Works on Flipkart and Croma. Amazon, Myntra, Blinkit and Reliance Digital are experimental. The panel tells you when the page you are on is supported.

How to use it
1. Open a product page on a supported store.
2. Click the PinScout icon to open the side panel.
3. Type your pincode and press Enter.
4. Watch the results come in and see the nearest place it can be delivered.

Want another store supported?
Open "Help add a new store" in the panel and follow the steps. PinScout records how that store checks delivery, hides tokens and personal details, and lets you email the recording to the developer from your own email app. Nothing is sent automatically.

Privacy
PinScout is free, needs no sign-up and shows no ads. It has no server and no analytics. Delivery checks go straight from your browser to the store you are on, and your home pincode is saved only on your device.

Pincode data: Department of Posts, Government of India, via data.gov.in (Government Open Data Licence – India). PinScout is an independent tool and is not affiliated with any shopping site.

Feedback: getsaurabhpagrut@gmail.com
```

## Getting people to use it (what actually moves installs)
1. Ask the first 20 to 30 users to leave a rating; the store ranks by ratings and weekly users far more than by text.
2. Post a short screen recording where deal hunters are: r/IndianGaming, r/india, r/IndiaTech, Telegram and WhatsApp deal groups, and around sale days (Big Billion Days, Great Indian Festival) when "not deliverable to your pincode" is most common.
3. Reply to reviews within a day; Google shows the developer response on the listing.
4. Add stores people request through the recorder and mention "now works on X" in each update.
