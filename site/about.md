# About

PinScout is made by **Saurabh Pagrut**, an independent developer in India.

It started with a familiar problem: a phone shows "not deliverable to your pincode", and the only way to find out where it *can* go is to type pincode after pincode into the store's delivery box. PinScout does that typing for you, in your own browser, and stops at the nearest place it can be delivered.

I build small, free tools that run on your device and collect nothing:

- **PinScout**: finds where an out-of-stock or undeliverable product can be delivered in India.
- **Bracel**: a virtual cat that lives on the edges of the websites you visit. https://bracel.saurabhpagrut.in
